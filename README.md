# Instrumentation in Practice

🇺🇸 English | 🇧🇷 [Português](#-português)

An open portfolio of small, progressive projects on **industrial instrumentation, process control and automation**, built with Python.

## About

I am a maintenance technician specializing in industrial instrumentation and an Electrical Engineering student. I work daily with transmitters, control valves, analyzers and control loops in the field. This repository is where I study the theory behind that practice, turning each concept into code that can be tested, validated and shared.

## Principles

- **Sources first.** Every formula, constant and concept is backed by a verifiable reference: textbooks, standards or peer-reviewed literature.
- **Validated results.** Every calculation is checked against worked examples from the literature or known physical values.
- **Synthetic data only.** No real plant data is used. All examples are generic.
- **Learning in public.** Each project documents the theory, the solution and what I learned.

## Princípios

- **Fonte primeiro.** Toda fórmula, constante e conceito tem referência verificável: livros, normas ou literatura revisada por pares.

- **Aprendendo em público.** Cada projeto documenta a teoria, a solução e o que aprendi.
